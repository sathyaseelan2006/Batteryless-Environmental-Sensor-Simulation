  [Google Shape;187;p2]:
    - ABSTRACT
  [Google Shape;188;p2]:
    - The Simulation-Based Eco-Driving Training System for Electric Vehicles is a software-based simulation developed to promote energy-efficient and environmentally responsible driving practices. The system allows users to control an electric vehicle using keyboard inputs and practice different driving conditions through a simple interactive simulation. It monitors parameters such as speed, battery level, energy consumption, acceleration, and braking behavior during driving. Based on the user's driving performance, the system calculates an eco-driving score and provides real-time feedback. The simulation demonstrates how smooth acceleration, controlled speed, and efficient braking can reduce energy consumption and improve battery performance. It provides a safe and risk-free platform for users to understand and practice eco-friendly driving techniques without using a real electric vehicle. By encouraging efficient driving habits, the system aims to reduce energy consumption, improve EV driving range, and support environmental sustainability.

  [Google Shape;209;p4]:
    - INTRODUCTION
  [Google Shape;210;p4]:
    - Electric vehicles (EVs) are an important solution for reducing fuel consumption, air pollution, and carbon emissions. However, driving behavior such as sudden acceleration, excessive speed, and harsh braking can increase energy consumption and reduce battery range. The Simulation-Based Eco-Driving Training System for Electric Vehicles is developed as a software-based platform to help users understand and practice energy-efficient driving techniques. The system monitors driving parameters and provides feedback to encourage safer, smoother, and more sustainable driving habits.

=== SLIDE 9 ===
  [Google Shape;264;p9]:
    - COMPONENTS DETAILS
  [Google Shape;265;p9]:
    - User Input Module – Accepts keyboard controls such as acceleration, braking, reversing, and steering to control the EV.
    - Vehicle Simulation Module – Simulates the movement and basic driving behavior of the electric vehicle using code.
    - Environment Module – Provides different road conditions such as city roads, highways, hills, and mountain roads along with weather conditions.
    - Vehicle Control Module – Manages vehicle speed, acceleration, braking, steering, and movement based on user input.
    - Monitoring Module – Monitors speed, battery level, distance travelled, energy consumption, acceleration, and braking behavior.
    - Energy Management Module – Calculates battery consumption according to vehicle speed, acceleration, braking, distance, and driving conditions.
    - Eco-Driving Score Module – Calculates the user's eco-driving score based on efficient speed control, smooth acceleration, braking, and energy usage.
    - Feedback Module – Displays the eco-driving score and provides suggestions to improve driving efficiency.
    - Data Storage Module – Stores driving session details, battery usage, energy consumption, scores, and performance statistics.
    - Output Module – Displays the final eco-driving score, battery usage, energy efficiency, driving performance, and estimated driving range.

=== SLIDE 12 ===
  [Title 1]:
    - MODULE DESCRIPTION
  [Text Placeholder 2]:
    - 1. Vehicle Simulation and City Environment Module
    - This module provides an interactive city-road environment for practising EV driving. It allows users to select an electric car or bike and perform basic operations such as acceleration, braking, steering, and reversing. The environment includes roads, traffic signals, pedestrians, zebra crossings, buildings, and other vehicles to create a realistic urban driving experience.
    - 2. Dynamic Weather and Driving Monitoring Module
    - This module simulates changing weather conditions, automatically transitioning from sunny to rainy weather during driving. It monitors important vehicle parameters such as speed, battery percentage, distance travelled, and eco score through a Heads-Up Display (HUD). It also helps users understand how weather conditions and driving behaviour affect road safety and battery consumption.
    - 3. Performance Evaluation and Feedback Module
    - This module evaluates the user's driving performance based on speed control, acceleration, braking, traffic-rule compliance, and battery usage. It calculates the safety and eco-driving scores and generates performance feedback. The feedback helps users identify driving mistakes, improve energy efficiency, and develop safe and responsible driving habits.

=== SLIDE 16 ===
  [Title 1]:
    - CONCLUSION
  [Text Placeholder 2]:
    - The Simulation-Based Eco-Driving Training System for Electric Vehicles was developed to help users learn safe and energy-efficient driving practices through a software-based simulation. The system provides an interactive city-road environment with essential driving controls such as acceleration, braking, steering, and reversing, along with traffic signals, pedestrians, road crossings, buildings, and other vehicles to create a realistic urban driving experience. It integrates a dynamic weather module that automatically changes environmental conditions between sunny and rainy weather during the simulation. A Heads-Up Display (HUD) provides essential driving information, including vehicle speed, battery percentage, eco score, and distance travelled. The system also monitors driving performance based on speed limits, safe driving practices, and battery consumption, helping users understand the importance of energy-efficient driving. Overall, the proposed system provides a practical and interactive platform for learning eco-driving techniques without requiring a physical electric vehicle or specialized simulation hardware. It helps users understand the relationship between driving behaviour, battery usage, road safety, and energy conservation while encouraging responsible driving habits and improving awareness of electric vehicle efficiency.
